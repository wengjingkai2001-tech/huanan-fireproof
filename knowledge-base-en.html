1. Core Concept Definitions

Fire-Rated WindowPer GB 16809, a window assembled from fire glass, frames and hardware that maintains fire integrity under standard fire test conditions. Classified by rating: Class A (1.50 h), Class B (1.00 h), Class C (0.50 h).Standard: GB 16809-2008
Fire Resistance RatingThe time (in hours) a building element maintains stability, integrity or insulation under standard fire test conditions. Common ratings: 0.5 h / 1.0 h / 1.5 h / 2.0 h / 3.0 h.Standards: GB/T 5907, GB 50016
Class A Fire Glass (Heat-Insulating)Fire glass meeting both fire integrity and heat insulation requirements. Huanan's composite heat-insulating fire glass is tested for 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 hour ratings.Standard: GB 15763.1-2009
Class C Fire Glass (Non-Insulating)Fire glass meeting fire integrity only (incl. monolithic fire glass). Huanan's nano crystal silicon, monolithic cesium-potassium and borosilicate fire glass (3 h) fall in this class.Standard: GB 15763.1-2009
Thermally Broken Aluminum WindowsEnergy-saving window system where PA66 nylon thermal-break strips separate inner and outer aluminum profiles to interrupt the heat bridge; combined with insulating glass (e.g. 6mm Low-E + 12A + 6mm) for lower U-values.Standard: GB/T 8478-2020
Insulating Glass Unit (IGU)Two or more panes separated by a spacer frame and hermetically sealed, filled with dry air or inert gas; combined with Low-E coating to reduce heat transfer coefficient (U-value).Standard: GB/T 11944-2012
Low-E GlassGlass coated with low-emissivity metal oxide films, produced by offline (magnetron sputtering) or online (pyrolytic) processes. Upstream cooperation: CSG & Xinyi offline Low-E distributor, Yaopi online Low-E Guangdong general agent.Process: magnetron sputtering / pyrolytic
Fire-Resistant Curtain WallCurtain wall system with fire glass and steel/aluminum frames meeting inter-story fire-stopping requirements, preventing fire and smoke spread along the building envelope.Standards: GB 50016, GB/T 21086

4. Field Experience & Pitfalls

Selection: How to set the fire-rated window class?

Per GB 50016, first determine the fire compartment and evacuation requirements, then choose: Class A (1.5h) for critical escape routes (stairwells, elevator lobbies); Class B (1.0h) for ordinary fire separation; Class C (0.5h) for auxiliary openings. Avoid defaulting to Class A everywhere — cost rises significantly.

Pitfall: Do not confuse fire glass Class A vs Class C

Class A (heat-insulating) meets both integrity and insulation; Class C (non-insulating) meets integrity only. If the design limits back-face temperature rise (hospitals, schools, refuge areas), Class A is mandatory. Verify the fire protection specification in drawings before ordering to avoid acceptance rework.

Pitfall: Fire window acceptance checklist

Verify three documents: 1) CCCF fire certification (or type test report) of the product; 2) fire glass test report matching the window class; 3) hardware certificates. The entity name on all documents must match the contract party.

Pitfall: Insulating glass and Low-E common mistakes

Offline Low-E coating must face the cavity (surface 2 or 3); reversed installation significantly degrades energy performance and cannot be reworked. PVB thickness determines safety rating — use 0.76mm or above for railings. Fire glass should not be re-cut; order to exact sizes.

Engineering: How to choose glass configuration?

Common configs in Huanan projects: curtain walls 6mm Low-E + 12A + 6mm insulated tempered; glass railings 6mm clear + 0.76PVB + 6mm laminated tempered; standard windows 5mm tempered. Fire-rated areas stack fire glass for combined energy + fire performance.

Supply chain: How is delivery stability ensured?

Huanan controls the chain from raw glass (CSG/Xinyi offline Low-E, Yaopi online Low-E via Guangdong Huanan New Materials) through base processing (Dongguan Xinyijia: tempering, insulating, laminating, fire glass), enabling predictable and committed lead times for large projects.
